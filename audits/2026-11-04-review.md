# Review of 2026-11-04: what to change in the extension

The review of the release of 2026-11-03 (extension 0.1.9 @ `f73d77a`, with the app 0.10.0 and page 0.14.0), made
right after it by two models of different providers (Claude and Codex), as Cross_Chain's review policy says
(`AUDIT.md`). Not a professional audit. The full reports stay private until the fixes are released; then
Cross_Chain's `AUDIT.md` gets the public summary. Cross_Chain's `WALLET-BACKLOG.md` tracks this file with
those of the other repositories.

No Critical or High finding on the extension. `ext/sign-plan.js` is shared with the app (`app/sign-plan.js`):
its changes are made in both, identical.

Status: **Proposed** (decision to confirm), **To do**, **Accepted**, **Done** (with the commit).

| ID | Sev. | What to change | Status |

| EXT-01 | Medium | **The signing window's isolation from the wallet page.** Today every extension page shares one origin, one storage and the passkey's RP ID, so the separation between the wallet page and the signing window is a convention of the code, not a boundary of the browser. Now: the README's claims corrected. Then, structural, before the extension leaves pre-release: the wallet page (with WalletKit and the decoders) as a manifest sandbox page, talking by messages to a small privileged host that forwards structured requests to the signing window, as the app's 0.10.0 split does. Reviewed after its release | Proposed |
| EXT-02 | Medium | **Typed data:** the primary type computed from `types` (what is hashed), the request's own `primaryType` refused when different; only the typed fields shown; the warnings (Permit, EIP-3009) on the computed type and the domain (`sign-plan.js`, and the page in Cross_Chain) | Proposed |
